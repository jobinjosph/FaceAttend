Simple workflow

From classroom entry to attendance record

The first version deliberately keeps recognition simple: students present themselves to the camera one at a time.

1

Faculty starts a period

Select semester, subject and period, then open an attendance session.

2

Student verifies face

The camera recognizes the registered student and checks the active session.

3

Entry or exit is saved

The faculty chooses Entry Mode or Exit Mode so the system never has to guess.

4

Faculty reviews attendance

Presence duration is calculated and the faculty confirms the final record.
